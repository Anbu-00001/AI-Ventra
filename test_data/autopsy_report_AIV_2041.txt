SECTION 9 — FORENSIC RECONSTRUCTION NOTES
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

Sequence of Events (Forensic Opinion):
1. Victim was administered benzodiazepines (likely covertly in drink)
   approximately 1.5-3 hours before death. This is consistent with
   the 10:30 PM restaurant visit — the drug was likely added to his
   beverage between 11:00 PM and midnight.

2. Victim was transported (voluntarily or otherwise) to the Service Road
   area in Whitefield. GPS and CCTV data should confirm route.

3. Victim was assaulted — initial defensive injuries suggest a brief
   period of resistance despite sedation. The broken fingernails and
   palm abrasion indicate a struggle.

4. Fatal blows were delivered to the cranial region with a heavy
   cylindrical object (pipe/rod). Minimum two distinct impacts.

5. Ligature strangulation was applied — possibly to ensure death
   after blunt force trauma.

6. Body was initially left face-down (accounting for anterior lividity),
   then repositioned to supine position. This indicates either:
   (a) the attacker returned to reposition, or
   (b) a second individual moved the body.

7. Body was partially concealed with construction debris —
   suggesting intent to delay discovery.

Key Forensic Indicators of Premeditation:
  - Benzodiazepine administration (planned sedation)
  - Weapon brought to scene (not weapon of opportunity)
  - Body repositioned and concealed post-mortem
  - Victim's phone and wallet removed (evidence destruction)

EVIDENCE PRESERVED:
  - Fingernail scrapings (DNA pending)

  - Blood samples (toxicology archive)
  - Clothing fibers (trace analysis pending)
  - Soil/debris samples from body and scene
